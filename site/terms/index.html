Terms and responsible use

Use Codex Account Switcher only with accounts you own or are authorized to access. The app changes the local Codex profile selected on your Mac; it does not grant account access, alter subscriptions, or override OpenAI policies.

Updated August 28, 2026 · Maintained by Zhao Liu (liuzhao1225) · MIT licensed

Which accounts may be used?

You are responsible for using only accounts that you own or have explicit permission to access. Do not use the app to bypass organization policy, account controls, billing rules, service limits, or another person's access restrictions.

What does the app change?

The app stores authorized local profile snapshots, changes the active file-backed Codex credential after user confirmation, verifies the selected identity, and reopens Codex Desktop. It does not merge accounts, combine workspaces, increase usage limits, move subscriptions, or revoke every server-side session.
